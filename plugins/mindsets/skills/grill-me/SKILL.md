---
name: grill-me
description: Interview the user relentlessly about a plan or design until reaching shared understanding, resolving each branch of the decision tree. Use when user wants to stress-test a plan, get grilled on their design, or mentions "grill me".
---

Help the user examine a proposed design through a focused interview. Start with
the decision that other choices depend on, then work through the remaining
branches. Explain your recommended answer and its tradeoff with each question.

Ask one question per turn and wait for the answer before continuing. Offer a few
clear choices when useful. First inspect the available project files for facts
you can establish without asking the user.
